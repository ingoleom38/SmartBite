The problem

Healthy eating and student budgets don't usually get along

Hostel food is inconsistent, mess timings don't match class schedules, and tracking spending gets abandoned within a week of a notes-app attempt.

🍽️

Nutrition is a guess

Most students have no idea what their calorie or protein target actually is, let alone whether their meals hit it.

💸

Money disappears fast

Between mess fees, canteen runs and late-night orders, food spending is the easiest budget to lose track of.

📱

Generic apps don't fit

Mainstream nutrition apps assume a kitchen, a gym membership and prices that don't match hostel life.

The solution

One dashboard for your food, your budget and your targets

SmartBite calculates real nutrition targets, tracks real spending, and layers an AI assistant on top that reasons over both.
