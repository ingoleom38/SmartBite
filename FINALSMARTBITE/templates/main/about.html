About the project

Why we built SmartBite

SmartBite started as a Data Science department project with one question: why do so few tools understand that a student's food choices are constrained by both nutrition and money at the same time?

Our approach

Instead of treating nutrition and budgeting as separate apps, SmartBite calculates real targets, tracks real spending, and connects the two through an AI assistant that reasons over your actual numbers, never invented ones.

Who it's for

Hostel and PG students juggling mess timings, canteen prices and a fixed monthly allowance, who want to eat well without spending hours planning it.
