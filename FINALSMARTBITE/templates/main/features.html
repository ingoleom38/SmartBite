Everything inside SmartBite

Features built around real student life

Every module below feeds the same student dashboard, and the same AI assistant, so nothing works in isolation.

🧮

Nutrition calculator

Enter age, gender, height, weight and activity level to get calorie, protein, carb and fat targets using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, logged automatically so you can track it over time.

📊

Expense tracking

Log food expenses by category (mess, canteen, groceries, eating out, snacks) with a note and date. Monthly totals and category breakdowns are computed straight from MySQL.

💰

Budget intelligence

Set a daily food budget and SmartBite works out your monthly budget, remaining balance, days left, and whether your current pace will put you over budget by month end.

🤖

AI nutrition assistant

SmartBite AI understands natural follow-ups ("make it cheaper", "no eggs") and always answers budget and nutrition questions from your real database numbers, never a guess.

🗓️

Personalized meal planning

Ask for a 1, 3 or 7-day plan built around your calorie/protein target, budget, diet preference and cooking access (no kitchen, induction only, or full kitchen).

🛒

Grocery planning

Turn any weekly budget into a shopping list of practical items with estimated prices, built to stay within what you asked to spend.

🔁

Food substitutions

"Replace eggs" or "cheaper alternative to chicken" returns options matched on protein, calories, cost and availability, not just a random swap.

📈

Nutrition & expense analytics

Trends for calorie and protein targets, monthly spending by category, and averages, all real, with an honest "not enough data yet" when history is thin.

💬

Chat history

Every conversation is saved per student and fully isolated, one student can never see another's messages, budget or nutrition data.
